CraftV with a friend: you both play Minecraft inside your own GTA V, in the same world
==================================================================================

Your friend sees you as your Minecraft character inside his GTA, and you see him in yours. Blocks either of you
places show up for both, and they're solid for both. Each of you has your own GTA (your own people and cars):
only the Minecraft part is shared.

The host (the one whose PC holds the world, Sary) changes nothing; they play as usual.

What the friend needs
---------------------
- GTA V Legacy (his own copy), story mode. Never take mods into GTA Online.
- Script Hook V, from the official site only: http://www.dev-c.com/gtav/scripthookv/
  (copy ScriptHookV.dll and dinput8.dll from its "bin" folder into the GTA V folder).
- Minecraft Java Edition (his own account) with Fabric for 26.3:
  a. Fabric Loader from the official site: https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/
     Run it, choose Minecraft 26.3, untick nothing, Install. A "fabric-loader-26.3" installation appears in the
     Minecraft Launcher.
  b. In the Minecraft Launcher: Installations > fabric-loader-26.3 > Edit > Game directory: pick a new, empty
     folder, for example C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Roaming\.minecraft-craftv
     (CraftV changes that Minecraft's settings: render distance, frame rate. Your normal Minecraft stays as it is.)
  c. Fabric API 0.161.0+26.3 from its official page: https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api (Versions tab, 26.3).
  d. Put the Fabric API jar and craftv-0.1.0.jar (in this folder) into that game directory's "mods" folder
     (create the folder if it isn't there).

Then the GTA part, exactly as the host did it: follow INSTALL.txt (CraftV.asi, CraftV.ini, ReShade, GTA settings).

Playing together
----------------
1. The host starts their Minecraft and GTA as usual. Their CraftV overlay (or F8 > "Friends join at") shows the
   address, for example 192.168.1.110:25565.
2. The friend starts the fabric-loader-26.3 installation in the Minecraft Launcher, then Multiplayer >
   Direct Connect > the host's address > Join Server. (To skip this every time, write the address after
   guest.join= in config\craftv.properties in that game directory; Minecraft then joins by itself.)
3. The friend starts GTA V and loads story mode. Their overlay says "MC view": their GTA now drives their
   Minecraft player, and they get Survival with the starter kit.

Not on the same Wi-Fi?
----------------------
The address above only works on the host's own network. Over the internet the host forwards TCP port 25565 on
their router to their PC (the router's "port forwarding" page) and gives the friend their public IP address with
:25565 at the end. Only share it with people you trust.

If something's wrong, send both sides' CraftV.log (next to GTA5.exe) and craftv-fabric.log (logs folder of the
Minecraft game directory).
